00 · Why

Design Philosophy

The tokens below are how. This is why — synthesized from Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, Linear's design method, and Stripe's design approach, each chosen for what it maps onto in an audit tool specifically.

01

Stripe

Show your work

A score, issue, or fix is never presented without its reasoning one click away. If a user can't see why Specd flagged something, the flag isn't finished.

02

Linear

Purpose-built, not configurable

Specd audits this specific thing in this specific way. Prefer the option that does one thing precisely over one that could theoretically do several things adequately.

03

Apple

Defer to the data

The audit data is the content. Chrome organizes it and never competes for attention. If an interface idea makes the data less scannable, the interface idea loses.

04

Apple + Stripe

Calm confidence, not alarm

A critical issue and a passed check sit in the same calm visual register. Severity is communicated once, clearly — never reinforced with urgency-signaling motion or color.
